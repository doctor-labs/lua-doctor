# 970 parenthesized condition

Severity: low · Confidence: medium · CWE: none

Opt-in: reported under `--category lint` or `--enable 95`.

## What it means

The whole condition of an `if`, `elseif`, `while`, or `until` is wrapped in parentheses. selene reports this as `parenthese_conditions`. The parentheses are redundant — `if (x)` is identical to `if x` — and they hide the condition's shape when it is later edited (adding `and` or `or` outside the parens does nothing useful, inside can change precedence). A parenthesized sub-expression inside a larger condition (`if (a or b) and c`) is not reported.

## Example

```lua
if (x) then
   f()
end
```

## How to fix

Remove the parentheses around the whole condition.

## Fix prompt

